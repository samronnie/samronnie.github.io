Good News
ReaderEdwin
Dear Samuel Collen Family, This Christmas season, as we come singing these carols, we’re happy to share the beautiful Good News with you. With all the celebrations and lights around us, we remember the true reason for Christmas — the birth of Jesus, who came to bring peace, love, and hope to the world. The Bible calls Him Immanuel, which means “God with us.” He didn’t remain far from us, but came near to be with us, to guide us, and to care for us in every season of life. As we sing tonight, may these songs remind you that Jesus is near — in your home and in your hearts. When life feels heavy, His love gives strength, and when things feel uncertain, His presence gives hope. We pray that your home and family will be filled with His peace and joy, not just this Christmas, but all through the coming year. 🎄 Merry Christmas! 🎄
